# What's new in TaniSim

The app's About page shows this list. Newest first; keep the `## <version> — <date>` heading format.

## 1.5.0 — 2026-11-25
- Calendar tab: bed sowing, transplanting and harvest dates from the Block C plan, nursery sowings, milestones, Ramadan and Raya, and the weekly rhythm, by month or as a list
- Add your own tasks to any day and tick them off
- About page with the app version, build, offline status and a Check for updates button

## 1.4.0 — 2026-11-25
- Item panel docked to the side, so the land stays in view while editing
- Land button on the toolbar for editing the land shape
- The ⋯ menu no longer gets cut off

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-24
- Fences tray: wooden, barbed-wire, electric, chain-link, BRC mesh, bamboo and picket fences, brick wall, living hedge and gate
- Boundary fence around the whole land, with perimeter and a post estimate

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-24
- Land of any shape: drag corners, add corners, see each side's length and the area
- New items: well, greenhouse, shade-net house, pondok, mushroom house, goat shed, water and fish tanks, durian, bamboo, gate
- Pitched roofs on buildings

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-24
- Layout tab: drag farm items onto a 1 m grid, Plan and 3D views, rotate, copy, resize, undo
- Block C starter layout, picture export and layout files

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24
- Installable app that works offline
- Today page with the weekly rhythm and upcoming milestones
- Farm notes with callouts, tables and diagrams, and links to the live graphics
