# 🌶️ Block C — Cash Beds Schedule (Dec 2026 – Aug 2027)

> [!info] What this is
> A bed-by-bed plan for **Block C** of the [[05 Kitchen Garden]]: the 11 short beds by the stall that grow for sale. The aim is **something to sell every week from January**, with **cili padi** as the long-running earner.
> Beds are numbered **C1–C11 from the stall back towards Block B**, so the chillies are the first thing customers see. Block C beds are ≈4.5–7 m long; this note assumes **≈6 m (≈7 m² of bed)** — scale plant counts to your real lengths.

> [!warning] One change to the build order
> [[05 Kitchen Garden]] builds the 10 beds nearest the hut first, which would leave Block C until April. To start earning in January, split December's 10 beds instead: **A1–A5 + C1, C2, C5, C6, C10**. Build **C3, C4, C7–C9, C11 in January**, and the rest of Blocks A and B by April as planned. Same workload — and five Block A beds of greens are plenty for 3 people at the start. Clear the scrub at the stall end first so these beds get full sun.

## 🌶️ Cili padi care (C1–C4)

- **Seed:** a packet of a named cili padi variety, or seed saved from a heavy-bearing plant you trust. Transplant at 5–6 weeks (4–6 true leaves).
- **Spacing:** 2 rows per bed, ≈60 cm apart in the row → ≈20 plants per 6 m bed, ≈70–80 plants across C1–C4.
- **Bed prep:** GML at the soil-test rate, 5–10 cm of compost, then mulch with rice husk or cut grass — mulch stops rain splashing soil onto the plants, which spreads anthracnose in the wet months.
- **Stake** every plant; loaded bushes fall over in storms.
- **Feed:** a handful of compost or chicken-manure compost per plant every month.
- **Pick every 5–7 days.** Ripe red sells higher than green; sell green when red gluts the market.
- **Pests & disease:** yellow sticky traps for thrips, whitefly and aphids; pull and bin any plant with mosaic or curled leaves; keep terung and tomato out of the beds next to C1–C4.
- **Replace** when yields drop (expect about a year or more). Replant on rotation beds, never chilli straight after chilli; stagger it — C1–C2 first, C3–C4 a month later — so picking never stops.

## 🧺 Selling it

- **Cili padi** in 100 g packs; **greens** in RM1.50–2 bundles; **ulam & serai** in small bundles ([[16 Surplus Sales & Trade]]).
- Fits the weekly rhythm: **Sun** sow · **Wed** post the harvest list · **Thu** pick greens & chilli · **Fri** deliver · **Sat** stall.

> - **Block A & B surplus greens** — sowing 2 beds a week grows far more than 3 people eat
> - **Eggs** from Jan 2027 ([[08 Poultry - Chickens & Ducks]])

> - **Rubber tapping** until felling in mid-2027
